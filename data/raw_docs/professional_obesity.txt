Obesity is a chronic multifactorial condition involving excess adiposity associated with increased cardiometabolic and mechanical risk. It is influenced by genetic, behavioural, environmental, endocrine, psychological, and socioeconomic factors. Complications include type 2 diabetes, hypertension, dyslipidaemia, obstructive sleep apnoea, osteoarthritis, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease, and cardiovascular disease. Assessment should include BMI, waist circumference, comorbidities, medication review, and lifestyle factors. Management includes behavioural intervention, dietary change, physical activity, pharmacotherapy, and bariatric referral where appropriate.
