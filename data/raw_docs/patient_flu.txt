Flu is a viral infection that can make people feel suddenly unwell. Symptoms often include a high temperature, body aches, tiredness, headache, sore throat, cough, and chills. Most people recover at home with rest, fluids, and simple pain relief. Flu can be more serious for older adults, pregnant people, young children, and people with long-term conditions. A flu vaccine can reduce the risk of serious illness. Medical advice should be sought if symptoms become severe or breathing becomes difficult.
