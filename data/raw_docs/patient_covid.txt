COVID-19 is an illness caused by a coronavirus. Symptoms can include a high temperature, cough, sore throat, tiredness, headache, loss of taste or smell, and breathing difficulty. Some people have mild symptoms, while others may become seriously unwell. Rest, fluids, and symptom relief can help mild cases. People at higher risk may need medical advice sooner. Good hygiene, vaccination, and staying away from others when unwell can reduce the spread of infection.
