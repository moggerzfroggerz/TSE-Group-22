Migraine is a type of headache that can cause severe throbbing pain, often on one side of the head. It may come with nausea, vomiting, sensitivity to light, or sensitivity to sound. Some people also experience warning signs called aura, such as flashing lights or visual changes. Migraine triggers can include stress, poor sleep, certain foods, dehydration, or hormonal changes. Treatment may include pain relief, anti-sickness medicine, avoiding triggers, and preventive medication if attacks are frequent.
