Asthma is a long-term condition that affects the airways in the lungs. It can make breathing harder and may cause coughing, wheezing, chest tightness, or shortness of breath. Symptoms can become worse at night, during exercise, or after triggers such as dust, smoke, pollen, pets, or cold air. Treatment usually includes inhalers. A reliever inhaler helps during symptoms, while a preventer inhaler reduces inflammation over time. People with asthma should follow their asthma action plan and seek urgent help if breathing becomes very difficult.
