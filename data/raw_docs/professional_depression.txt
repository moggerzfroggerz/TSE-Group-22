Depression is a mood disorder characterised by persistent low mood, anhedonia, reduced energy, cognitive changes, sleep disturbance, appetite change, feelings of guilt or worthlessness, and possible suicidal ideation. Assessment should consider duration, severity, functional impairment, risk, comorbid anxiety, substance use, medical causes, and medication effects. Management may include psychoeducation, psychological therapies such as CBT, antidepressant medication, social support, safety planning, and regular review. Urgent escalation is required where there is immediate risk of self-harm or suicide.
