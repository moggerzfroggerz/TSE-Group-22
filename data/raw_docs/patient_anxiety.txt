Anxiety is a feeling of worry, fear, or nervousness that can become difficult to control. It may cause physical symptoms such as a fast heartbeat, sweating, shaking, stomach discomfort, poor sleep, or shortness of breath. Some anxiety is normal, but persistent anxiety can affect daily life. Support may include talking therapies, breathing techniques, lifestyle changes, and medication in some cases. People should seek help if anxiety becomes overwhelming or affects work, study, relationships, or safety.
