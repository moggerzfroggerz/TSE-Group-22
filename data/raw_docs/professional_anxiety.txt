Anxiety disorders involve excessive fear, worry, or avoidance causing functional impairment. Presentations include generalised anxiety disorder, panic disorder, phobias, social anxiety, and anxiety related to trauma or medical conditions. Symptoms may include autonomic arousal, tachycardia, sweating, tremor, gastrointestinal disturbance, insomnia, and cognitive rumination. Assessment should consider duration, severity, triggers, comorbid depression, substance use, and risk. Management may include psychoeducation, cognitive behavioural therapy, lifestyle interventions, and pharmacological treatment such as SSRIs depending on clinical need.
