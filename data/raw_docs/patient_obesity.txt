Obesity means having excess body fat that may affect health. It can increase the risk of type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease, joint pain, sleep apnoea, and some cancers. Causes can include diet, activity levels, genetics, medication, mental health, and environment. Management usually involves gradual lifestyle changes such as healthier eating, regular physical activity, sleep improvement, and professional support. The aim is not only weight loss but also better overall health and reduced risk.
