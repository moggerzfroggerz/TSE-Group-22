Diabetes mellitus is a metabolic disorder characterised by chronic hyperglycaemia resulting from impaired insulin secretion, insulin resistance, or both. Type 1 diabetes is autoimmune beta-cell destruction requiring insulin therapy. Type 2 diabetes is commonly associated with insulin resistance, obesity, and progressive beta-cell dysfunction. Diagnosis is based on HbA1c, fasting plasma glucose, oral glucose tolerance testing, or random glucose with symptoms. Management includes lifestyle intervention, glycaemic monitoring, metformin as common first-line therapy in type 2 diabetes, and escalation based on cardiovascular, renal, and individual risk factors.
