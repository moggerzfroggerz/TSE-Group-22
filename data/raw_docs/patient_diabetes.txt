Diabetes is a condition where the level of sugar in the blood becomes too high. This can happen because the body does not produce enough insulin or because insulin does not work properly. Symptoms may include feeling very thirsty, passing urine more often, tiredness, weight changes, and blurred vision. Diabetes can be managed with healthy eating, regular activity, blood sugar checks, and medication if needed. If it is not managed well, it can lead to problems with the heart, kidneys, eyes, or nerves.
