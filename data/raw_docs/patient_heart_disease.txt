Heart disease refers to conditions that affect the heart and blood vessels. It can include narrowed arteries, chest pain, heart attacks, or heart failure. Risk factors include smoking, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, unhealthy diet, lack of exercise, and family history. Symptoms may include chest pain, breathlessness, tiredness, or swelling in the legs. Lifestyle changes and medication can reduce risks. Sudden chest pain or severe breathlessness should be treated as urgent.
