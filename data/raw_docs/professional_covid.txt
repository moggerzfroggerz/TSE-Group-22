COVID-19 is caused by SARS-CoV-2 and ranges from asymptomatic infection to severe viral pneumonia, acute respiratory distress syndrome, thromboembolic complications, and multisystem involvement. Clinical features may include fever, cough, anosmia, fatigue, sore throat, and dyspnoea. Risk stratification is important for older adults, immunocompromised individuals, pregnant patients, and those with comorbidities. Management depends on disease severity and may include supportive care, oxygen therapy, antivirals, corticosteroids, or hospital escalation. Vaccination reduces severe disease risk.
