Migraine is a primary headache disorder involving recurrent attacks of moderate to severe headache, commonly unilateral and pulsating, with associated nausea, photophobia, phonophobia, and sometimes aura. Pathophysiology involves trigeminovascular activation, neurogenic inflammation, cortical spreading depression in aura, and altered pain modulation. Acute management may include simple analgesia, NSAIDs, triptans, and antiemetics. Preventive therapy can be considered when attacks are frequent or disabling. Assessment should identify red flags, medication overuse headache, triggers, and impact on function.
