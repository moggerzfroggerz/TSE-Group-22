Influenza is an acute respiratory viral infection caused mainly by influenza A or B viruses. It presents with abrupt onset fever, myalgia, headache, malaise, cough, sore throat, and respiratory symptoms. Complications include viral pneumonia, secondary bacterial pneumonia, exacerbation of chronic disease, and severe illness in high-risk groups. Management is usually supportive, although antivirals may be considered for selected high-risk or severe cases. Annual vaccination remains a key public health intervention due to antigenic drift and seasonal circulation.
