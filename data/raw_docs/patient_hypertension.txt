High blood pressure, also called hypertension, means the force of blood against the artery walls is consistently too high. Many people do not notice symptoms, so it is often found during routine checks. If untreated, it can increase the risk of heart disease, stroke, kidney problems, and other serious conditions. Management may include reducing salt intake, eating a balanced diet, exercising regularly, limiting alcohol, stopping smoking, and taking prescribed medication. Regular blood pressure checks are important.
