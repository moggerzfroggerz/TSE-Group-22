Cardiovascular disease includes coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, valvular disorders, and peripheral vascular disease. Coronary artery disease commonly results from atherosclerotic plaque formation causing myocardial ischaemia. Risk factors include hypertension, dyslipidaemia, diabetes, smoking, obesity, sedentary lifestyle, and family history. Clinical presentation may include angina, dyspnoea, palpitations, syncope, or acute coronary syndrome. Management involves risk modification, pharmacotherapy such as antiplatelets or statins where indicated, and procedural intervention in selected cases.
