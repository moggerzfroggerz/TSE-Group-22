Hypertension is persistent elevation of arterial blood pressure and is a major modifiable risk factor for cardiovascular, cerebrovascular, and renal disease. Diagnosis typically requires repeated clinic or ambulatory or home blood pressure measurements. Pathophysiology may involve increased systemic vascular resistance, renal sodium handling, sympathetic activation, and vascular remodelling. Management includes lifestyle modification and pharmacological therapy guided by age, ethnicity, comorbidities, and cardiovascular risk. Treatment targets aim to reduce long-term complications such as myocardial infarction, stroke, heart failure, and chronic kidney disease.
