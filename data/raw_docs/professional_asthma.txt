Asthma is a chronic inflammatory airway disorder characterised by variable expiratory airflow limitation, bronchial hyperresponsiveness, and recurrent symptoms including wheeze, cough, chest tightness, and dyspnoea. Pathophysiology involves airway inflammation, smooth muscle contraction, mucus hypersecretion, and reversible airway narrowing. Management commonly includes short-acting beta agonists for symptom relief and inhaled corticosteroids for long-term control. Escalation may involve LABA therapy, leukotriene receptor antagonists, or biologics depending on severity and phenotype. Clinical review should assess control, inhaler technique, triggers, adherence, and exacerbation risk.
