Asthma is a long-term condition that affects the airways in the lungs. It causes the airways to become inflamed and narrow, making it difficult to breathe. Common symptoms include wheezing, coughing, chest tightness, and shortness of breath. These symptoms can vary in severity and may worsen at night or during physical activity. Asthma triggers can include allergens such as pollen, dust mites, pet dander, and environmental factors like cold air or pollution.

Treatment for asthma typically involves the use of inhalers. Reliever inhalers help to quickly open the airways during an asthma attack, while preventer inhalers are used daily to reduce inflammation and prevent symptoms. In some cases, additional medications may be prescribed. Managing asthma also involves avoiding known triggers and monitoring symptoms regularly.
